## Summary

This dataset is a compilation of the voting data related to the resolutions adopted by the General Assembly at its regular, special, emergency special sessions from its inception until resolution 81/1 adopted on 17 September 2026. It is derived from the UN Digital Library voting records and provides information on the outcome of each resolution, including its modality of adoption and, where applicable, the recorded vote count. The dataset comprises 20,834 entries, with each entry representing one adopted resolution.

## Data dictionary

* `resolution`: Symbol of the adopted resolution.
* `session`: Session of the General Assembly. (2 empty rows).
* `date`: Date of the vote.
* `title`: Title of the resolution.
* `modality`: Indicates how the resolution was adopted: vote, recorded (resolution adopted through a vote, individual votes of Member States are recorded); vote, non-recorded (resolution adopted through a vote, individual votes not recorded); without vote (resolution adopted without a vote).
* `draft`: Symbol of the draft resolution. (11,054 empty rows).
* `amended_draft`: Records amendments to draft resolution; symbol of the draft resolution as amended by the document recorded under 'draft'. (20,830 empty rows).
* `committee_report`: Report of the main General Assembly Committee, transmitting texts of draft resolutions. (3,592 empty rows).
* `meeting`: Symbol of the meeting record during which the resolution was adopted.
* `agenda_title`: Title of the agenda item. (383 empty rows).
* `subjects`: Topics associated with each agenda item. They may be a single concept or be combined with other concepts or qualifying information. (6,717 empty rows).
* `vote_note`: Additional voting information; used, for instance, to record peculiarities. (20,824 empty rows).
* `total_yes`: Number of "yes" votes (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote). (15,104 empty rows).
* `total_no`: Number of "no" votes (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote). (15,104 empty rows).
* `total_abstentions`: Number of abstentions (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote). (15,104 empty rows).
* `total_non_voting`: Number of non-voting members (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote). (15,104 empty rows).
* `total_ms`: Total number of Member States (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote). (15,104 empty rows).
* `undl_link`: Link to the voting record in the UN Digital Library.
