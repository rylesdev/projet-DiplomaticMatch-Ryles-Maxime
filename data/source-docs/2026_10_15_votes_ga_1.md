## Summary

This dataset is a compilation of the voting data related to the resolutions adopted by the General Assembly at its regular, special, emergency special sessions from its inception until resolution 81/1 adopted on 17 September 2026. It is derived from the UN Digital Library voting records and provides information on Member States' votes related to resolutions adopted through a recorded vote. Votes on individual paragraphs of draft resolutions or drafts that failed to be adopted are not included. The dataset comprises 954,382 entries, with each entry representing the vote of one Member State on a particular resolution.

## Data dictionary

* `ms_code`: Member State three-letter code.
* `ms_name`: Member State official name at the time of the vote.
* `ms_vote`: Member State vote (Y for yes, N for no, A for abstention, X for non-voting).
* `date`: Date of the vote.
* `session`: Session of the General Assembly. (386 empty rows).
* `resolution`: Symbol of the adopted resolution.
* `draft`: Symbol of the draft resolution. (436,168 empty rows).
* `committee_report`: Report of the main General Assembly Committee, transmitting texts of draft resolutions. (196,167 empty rows).
* `meeting`: Symbol of the meeting record during which the resolution was adopted.
* `title`: Title of the resolution.
* `agenda_title`: Title of the agenda item. (12,249 empty rows).
* `subjects`: Topics associated with each agenda item. They may be a single concept or be combined with other concepts or qualifying information. (234,818 empty rows).
* `vote_note`: Additional voting information; used, for instance, to record peculiarities. (954,023 empty rows).
* `total_yes`: Number of "yes" votes (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote).
* `total_no`: Number of "no" votes (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote).
* `total_abstentions`: Number of abstentions (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote).
* `total_non_voting`: Number of non-voting members (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote).
* `total_ms`: Total number of Member States (completed only for resolutions adopted by recorded vote).
* `undl_link`: Link to the voting record in the UN Digital Library.
